Frequently Asked

Your Questions, Answered

How long does a bin cleaning take?

+
Most single bin cleanings take between 10–20 minutes. Full property packages may take 30–45 minutes depending on the number of bins.

Are your cleaning products safe for children and pets?

+
Absolutely. We use high-performance, eco-friendly solutions that are non-toxic and biodegradable — completely safe for your family, pets, and surrounding environment.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

+
No, you don't need to be home. Just leave your bin in an accessible location and we'll handle everything. We'll notify you when the job is complete.

How often should I have my bins cleaned?

+
We recommend monthly cleanings, especially in Las Vegas where heat accelerates bacteria and odor growth. However, bi-monthly is a great option for lighter usage.

What areas in Las Vegas do you serve?

+
We proudly serve the greater Las Vegas area including Summerlin, Henderson, North Las Vegas, and surrounding neighborhoods. Contact us to confirm your address.

How do I book an appointment?

+
Simply fill out the contact form below, call us, or use our online booking link. We'll confirm your appointment within 24 hours.
